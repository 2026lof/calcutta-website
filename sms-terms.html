Text Club Terms

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Program description

The Calcutta Wrap & Roll Text Club sends recurring marketing text messages about specials, new menu items, discounts, events, and catering offers. Sign up on our website or by responding to one of our ads or forms. You may also receive a one-time confirmation message when you join.

Key details

  • Message frequency: up to 4 messages per month.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply. There is no other charge for joining.
  • To opt out: reply STOP to any message. You'll receive one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed. You can also reply CANCEL, END, QUIT, or UNSUBSCRIBE.
  • For help: reply HELP to any message, or contact us at (914) 591-2222 or hello@calcuttawrapandroll.com.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase. You do not have to join the Text Club to order from us.
  • Eligibility: you must be 18 or older and the account holder of the mobile number, or have the account holder's permission.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission from your carrier.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy to learn how we handle your information.

Changes

We may change or end the Text Club at any time. We will post updates to these terms on this page.
