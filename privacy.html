Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SMS Foods LLC, doing business as Calcutta Wrap & Roll ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, join RestroBucks or our email list, contact us, or interact with us on social media.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, mobile number, email address, and message when you sign up for offers, fill out a form, or contact us.
  • Social media and ads: if you respond to one of our Facebook or Instagram ads or forms, we receive the details you choose to submit, such as your name, phone number, and email.
  • Automatic information: basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We may use cookies and tools such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use our site and to measure our ads.

Online orders are placed through our ordering partner at ordercalcuttawrapandroll.com, whose own privacy policy applies to payment and order details.

How we use information

  • To send you specials, offers, event news, and updates you signed up for.
  • To answer your questions and catering requests.
  • To improve our website, menu, and marketing.
  • To keep records of your consent and opt-out choices, and to comply with the law.

Text messages (SMS)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We use service providers only to deliver our messages. You can reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our RestroBucks Text Terms for details.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as our website host, our marketing agency (Panoramic Nuggets), and our text messaging and email delivery providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights and safety.

Your choices

  • Texts: reply STOP to any message.
  • Emails: click "unsubscribe" at the bottom of any email.
  • Access or deletion: email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as you remain subscribed or as needed for the purposes above, and use reasonable safeguards to protect it. We keep records of opt-outs so we can continue to honor them.

Children

Our marketing programs are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new effective date.
