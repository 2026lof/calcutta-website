If you're vegan or avoiding gluten, eating out can mean a lot of questions and a sad side salad. Indian food is different. Some of its best-loved dishes are vegan by nature, and most curries are eaten with rice, not bread. Here's what to order from our kitchen in Ardsley.

Our vegan menu

These dishes are on the vegan section of our menu:

  • Chana Masala: chickpeas in a tangy tomato and onion sauce. Our top pick.
  • Chana Saag: chickpeas with spinach.
  • Rajma: red kidney beans, Punjabi comfort food.
  • Dal Tadka and Dal Bukhara: lentils, finished with spices sizzled in oil.
  • Aloo Gobi, Aloo Bhindi, Aloo Tamatar and Aloo Palak (No Cream): potatoes with cauliflower, okra, tomato or spinach.
  • Baingan Bhartha: smoky roasted eggplant.
  • Vegetable Kadai: mixed vegetables with peppers and onion.
  • Vegetable Biryani (No Butter) and Jeera Rice
  • Butter-Free Dosas and Idli from the South Indian side of our kitchen
  • Vegetable Samosas, Papad and Fresh Calcutta Salad

A vegan dinner for two: Chana Masala, Baingan Bhartha, Dal Tadka and Jeera Rice, with Vegetable Samosas to start.

Avoiding gluten

Most of our vegetable, bean and lentil dishes are gluten-free, and they're served with rice instead of bread. Dishes with paneer, chicken, lamb, goat or seafood are a different story, so ask us about those first.

  • Choose: vegetable, bean and lentil curries like Chana Masala, Rajma, Aloo Gobi, Baingan Bhartha and dal, with rice.
  • Ask first: anything with paneer or meat.
  • Skip: naan, paratha (including our kathi rolls) and samosas, which are made with wheat flour.

If you have celiac disease or a serious allergy, please tell us when you order. Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy and nuts, so we'll walk you through the safest choices.
